Last updated: May 16, 2026

58Labs operations ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Pisigo mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Data Collection and Use

To provide a better experience and maintain our free services, we use third-party services (Google AdMob) that may collect information used to identify you.

  • Device Identifier: We collect device identifiers to handle Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and manage ad serving.

  • Crash Data: We collect performance and crash logs to monitor technical errors and improve game stability.

2. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14 and above, we request your permission to track your activity across other apps for advertising purposes. If you opt-out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

3. Security

We value your trust in providing us your Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.
